#  Problem Statement

The custom PC building market involves thousands of possible hardware combinations, each greatly affecting the final price. For customers and PC technicians, estimating an accurate total build cost is often challenging due to several factors.

- Hardware prices vary significantly across brands, models, and generations.
- Certain components heavily influence total cost (CPU, GPU, PSU), while others have a minimal effect — making manual estimation difficult.
- Customers often struggle to know whether a configuration fits within their budget.
- Comparing multiple custom build options becomes confusing without expert support.
- Lack of transparency or price predictability often leads to uncertainty and delayed decision-making.

## Challenges for PC Shops & Technicians

- Price estimation is mostly done manually, by individually adding component prices.
- When customers change the configuration (upgrade RAM, change GPU, switch PSU, etc.), the entire quotation must be recalculated from scratch.
- If the customer's budget is exceeded, the technician must:
  - Modify multiple components
  - Recalculate prices
  - Regenerate the quotation
  - Repeat this process multiple times
  
 This becomes a time-consuming, repetitive, and manpower-heavy task, especially during high customer traffic.

### Manual Calculation Issues

- Higher chance of calculation errors
- Incorrect quotations
- Customer dissatisfaction
- Miscommunication among staff
