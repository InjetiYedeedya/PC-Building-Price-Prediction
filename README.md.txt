# PC Building Price Prediction

## Problem Statement

The custom PC building market involves thousands of possible hardware combinations, each greatly affecting the final price. For customers, estimating the total build cost becomes difficult because:

- Hardware prices vary across brands, models, and generations.

- Some components influence price heavily (CPU, GPU, PSU), while others contribute      minimally — making it hard to predict total cost.

- Customers often don’t know whether their chosen configuration fits their budget.

- Comparing multiple configuration options becomes confusing without an expert.

- Price estimation is usually done manually, by adding individual part costs.

- If the customer wants to change the configuration (e.g., upgrade RAM, downgrade GPU, change PSU), the entire quotation must be remade from scratch.

- In many cases, if the customer says the price is above their budget, the technician must manually:

     - change 2–3 components,

     - recalculate the total,

     - regenerate the quotation,

     - and repeat this multiple times.

- This becomes a time-consuming, repetitive, and manpower-intensive process — especially during busy hours.

- Human calculation errors may lead to:

   - Wrong quotations

   - Customer dissatisfaction

   - Miscommunication within the shop


### Why This System Is Needed

- Instantly estimating total build cost

- Allowing customers to modify configurations freely

- Removing the need for manual recalculation

- Reducing technician workload

- Improving accuracy and consistency

- Enhancing customer satisfaction and business efficiency
